Dashboard Design System

Interactive documentation for our design language. Based on the 9-element framework, focusing on clean data visualization, neutral scales, and AI-driven accents.

✨

Tech-Forward

Emphasizes efficiency and modern SaaS aesthetics with subtle AI gradients.

⚖️

Professional

High contrast typography and strict neutral palettes reduce cognitive load.

🍃

Lightweight

Ample whitespace and flat surfaces (Level 0) create a breathable interface.
